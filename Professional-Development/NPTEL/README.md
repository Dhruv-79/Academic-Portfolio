# NPTEL Certifications

This directory contains official certifications, credentials, and verification records earned through NPTEL (National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning) courses funded by the Ministry of Education, Govt. of India.

---

## 📌 Overview

The documentation and proof of completion stored here demonstrate continuous learning, academic rigor, and domain expertise gained through structured online courses conducted by premier Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) and IISc.

**Key Areas Covered**

* **Core Computer Science & Engineering:** Software engineering, advanced programming, data structures, algorithms, and core domain foundations.
* **Modern Technical Innovations:** Emerging paradigms including cloud computing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and emerging digital technologies.
* **Management & Strategy:** Entrepreneurship development, business fundamentals, strategic decision-making, and venture lifecycle management.
