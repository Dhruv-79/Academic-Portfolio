📚 Subject / Learning Area
The credential represents completion of the UGC NEP 2020 Orientation & Sensitization Programme organized under the Malaviya Mission Teacher Training initiative[cite: 8]. The programme provides structured exposure to the reforms, pedagogical framework, and strategic goals outlined in the National Education Policy 2020.
The certificate specifically associates the learning with the following areas:
National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 · Academic Reform · Higher Education Pedagogy · Multidisciplinary Curriculum Design · Governance & Educational Policy

🎯 Key Learning
Through this learning programme, I developed a foundational understanding of:
* Core vision, goals, and key pillars of the National Education Policy 2020.
* Implementation strategies for multidisciplinary and holistic education models in higher education.
* Frameworks for outcome-based education, credit transfer systems, and flexible learning pathways.
* Integrating technology, research innovation, and Indian Knowledge Systems into contemporary curricula.
* Institutional governance, quality assurance, and continuous professional development practices for educators.
