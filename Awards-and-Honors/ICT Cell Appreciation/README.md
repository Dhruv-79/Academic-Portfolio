# 📖 About the Recognition

The **ICT Cell Certificate of Appreciation** was awarded in recognition of my contributions as an **ICT Coordinator** at the **Parul Institute of Computer Applications (PICA), Parul University**.

In this role, I served as a bridge between the university's Information & Communication Technology Cell, faculty members, and students by facilitating the adoption of institutional digital technologies and supporting the implementation of university-wide academic initiatives.

The recognition reflects my commitment to promoting technology-enabled education, strengthening digital learning practices, and supporting faculty members in effectively utilizing institutional learning platforms.

---

# 🎯 Key Responsibilities

✔ Coordinated ICT-related academic activities within the department.

✔ Trained faculty members to effectively use the **Parul University Learning Management System (PULMS).**

✔ Guided faculty in creating complete course structures according to university academic guidelines.

✔ Assisted faculty in uploading learning materials, assessments, attendance, and course resources.

✔ Provided technical support to students regarding LMS-related issues.

✔ Worked closely with the ICT Cell during implementation of university technology initiatives.

✔ Helped improve faculty adoption of digital teaching methodologies.

✔ Supported smooth execution of online academic workflows.

---

# 🚀 Major Contributions

### Faculty Development

Conducted continuous guidance sessions for faculty members to improve their understanding of institutional digital platforms and online teaching practices.

### Learning Management System Support

Facilitated effective implementation of the university LMS by assisting faculty in course creation, academic resource management, and digital content organization.

### Student Assistance

Provided first-level technical guidance to students experiencing difficulties while accessing or using university digital services.

### Academic Digital Transformation

Contributed toward enhancing technology adoption across the department through proactive support, coordination, and problem solving.
